METHODS & EVIDENCE

Understand the result before you use it.

Fifteen paper-inspired Indian stock portfolios. Recorded simulations through 22 October 2026. Open code and explicit assumptions.

Exploratory research.

The current universe creates survivorship bias. 2018–2025 influenced selection. The 2026 comparison has been inspected repeatedly and is not an untouched holdout.

What was tested

169 predefined parameter combinations across six rounds. Each family was selected by its minimum Calmar score across independently restarted 2018–2022 development and 2023–2025 validation windows. The website displays frozen selected configurations. Both displayed periods start independently in cash; 2026 does not inherit the earlier portfolio.

The universe matters

The current Nifty500-derived snapshot mapped 500 stocks. Nine retrospective exclusions left 491. Historical exits and failed companies are absent, industry labels are current, and broker-adjusted prices were not independently reconstructed from corporate actions. Sparse shared-calendar sessions were omitted. A fresh broker download or different universe can produce different results.

How positions are simulated

Starting equity is normalized to 100 with fractional adjusted shares and no leverage. Review signals use completed sessions. Removed stocks sell at the next available open; additions buy three sessions after the signal, including the initial allocation. Sale proceeds remain unsettled for two sessions. New positions receive signal equity divided by the configured basket size. Retained holdings are not resized, so weights drift.

No intramonth stops or replacements are added. Missing entry opens prevent buys; unavailable sells retry. Terminal holdings are liquidated at an estimated final adjusted close. Whole-day volume and, in stress tests, daily high/low are execution feasibility checks known after the open, not beforehand. This is an idealized historical model.

Charges and slippage

The baseline uses buy charges of 0.1187406%, sell charges of 0.1037406%, and adverse slippage of 0.05% per side. Current proportional delivery rates are applied uniformly across history. Fixed DP/account fees, historical tariff changes, dividend cash flows, delisting proceeds, personal taxes and market impact are omitted. Changing data broker does not change these frozen fee assumptions automatically.

Round 1's recorded stress doubles slippage. Later rounds also restrict one-price candles, changing which fills occur. Stress can occasionally improve the simulated path and is not a guaranteed lower bound.

Read the numbers consistently

Total return is compounded gain. CAGR annualizes over elapsed calendar time. Drawdown measures loss from the preceding equity peak, including initial equity 100. Sharpe uses daily returns, zero cash interest and 252-session annualization. Annual returns within 2018–2025 follow a continuous portfolio; 2026 is a fresh-start test. Trade contributions are normalized equity points, not rupees. Completed positions include estimated terminal exits.

Reproduce locally

Download a strategy ZIP and give its SKILL.md to your AI. It asks for the broker, launches the local login form, downloads authorised history with pacing and runs the Python implementation. Exact agreement requires the same dataset snapshot; cross-broker runs are independent replications.

The public website receives no credentials and contains no raw broker price cache. Keys and tokens are entered in the local helper, kept out of chat and normal logs, and cleared when that process exits. An unrestricted agent on the same computer is not cryptographically isolated from local processes.

Data access and limits

Broker history depends on account entitlements. The helper paces below documented ceilings, caches completed chunks and bounds retries. API access does not automatically grant public redistribution rights.

  • Zerodha history: 3 requests/second.
  • Dhan data: 5 requests/second and 100,000/day.
  • FYERS Standard: 5/second, 50/minute, 5,000/day. Prime: 10/second, 500/minute, 500,000/day. Standard daily history is a rolling five years and cannot reproduce the full original period.

Limits checked 24 October 2026; recheck your plan. Other applications consume the same broker limits. The helper exposes history, not trading endpoints.

New signal windows

Intermediate-horizon momentum ranks the return from calendar month-end twelve months ago to six months ago. High-volume premium requires month-end share volume above its prior 100-session 90th percentile, excluding that day. Seasonality averages the next holding month’s returns over up to five prior years, with at least two complete observations.

Intermediate momentum’s 2018–2025 return falls from 1209.07% to 904.25% under doubled slippage and one-price-bar restrictions. Volume corporate-action changes are not independently reconstructed. Early seasonal rankings have particularly few prior years.

Source and verification

Results come from the supplied 12-strategy research handoff. The 27 October supplement adds three strategies and 18 configurations. Original paper links and adaptations appear on each strategy. Source hashes identify imported artifacts. Software checks verify arithmetic and reproduction; they do not remove data bias or establish future profitability.
