# Calibration meeting pack

## Use
Use before a human-led performance calibration meeting to make evidence comparable. Do not allocate ratings, impose a distribution or select promotion candidates.

## Inputs
Role-level criteria, published rating anchors if used, reviewer-proposed assessments, evidence and review period. Optional `role-brief.md` defines scope; private `hr-context.md` defines pack format and authorized audience.

## Do
1. Group comparable role scopes and periods. Record changes in responsibilities or opportunities to demonstrate work without inferring personal circumstances.
2. Check every reviewer claim against the stated criterion and cited example. Mark unsupported claims and missing evidence; do not fill them with guesses.
3. Create a neutral evidence summary per review: outputs, context, examples, contradictory evidence and employee response. Preserve the reviewer's proposed assessment as attributed input.
4. Surface inconsistent anchor use, duplicate evidence, recency concentration or different standards for similar work as questions. Do not “correct” a rating or calculate a forced ranking.
5. Build an agenda around questions requiring clarification. Provide a decision log for human participants; record their conclusion, rationale and owner only when supplied.
6. Keep private evidence packets separate from the meeting's administrative status summary.

## Deliver
`calibration-pack.md` grouped by role scope.
`calibration-questions.csv`: review_id, criterion, reviewer_claim, evidence_refs, gap_or_conflict, clarification_owner.
`decision-log.csv`: review_id, human_decision, rationale, evidence_refs, approver, decided_at, follow_up.

## Check
Every claim is traceable, proposed assessments remain attributed, and unresolved questions are not silently converted to outcomes.

## Example
“Prepare calibration questions from these manager reviews and our level framework; retain each manager's proposal without suggesting final ratings.”
