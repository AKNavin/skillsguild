# Hiring manager intake

## Inputs
Use the request, business outcome, hiring urgency, manager, budget and team context. Read optional private `hr-context.md` for working style and existing `role-brief.md` for prior agreements; distinguish current approvals from old assumptions.

## Workflow
1. Extract the problem this hire must solve, work currently uncovered, reporting line, decision authority and collaborators. Separate new headcount from replacement and confirm approval status.
2. Translate "senior", "strong communicator" or "culture fit" into observable work: decisions owned, scale, stakeholders and deliverables. Ask only for missing facts that change the search or assessment; draft the rest with labelled open items.
3. Define outcomes for the first 90 days and first year. Identify genuinely essential entry requirements, skills that can be learned and preferences. Challenge degree, tenure or prestige filters without an evidenced role need.
4. Record location, working arrangement, travel, hours, compensation currency/range, employment type and reasonable scheduling constraints. Do not guess legal eligibility or invent benefits.
5. Propose the assessment stages, job-related evidence each stage gathers, accountable reviewer, response deadlines and target start date. Show the manager a compact decision list for unresolved tradeoffs.

## Deliver
A private `role-brief.md` containing `role_id, version, owner, approval_status, purpose, outcomes, scope, essentials, learnable_skills, constraints, budget, assessment_plan, source_notes, open_decisions`. Include a one-page intake summary and next actions. If updating an existing brief, show changed facts and their sources.

## Check
Each requirement maps to actual work. Requirements, salary and working arrangement agree across the brief; missing approval is not marked approved. Style preferences may change presentation, never job standards.

Example: "Turn these manager notes into a role brief for our first customer success lead and highlight the decisions still needed."

Sources: [CIPD recruitment process](https://www.cipd.org/uk/knowledge/factsheets/recruitment-factsheet/) · [CIPD job design](https://prod.cipd.org/uk/knowledge/factsheets/job-design-factsheet/).
