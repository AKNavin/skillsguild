# Candidate update desk

## Inputs
Use candidate ID, verified stage, event or recorded human decision, next step and contact details. Optional private `hr-context.md` supplies approved voice; `role-brief.md` supplies role/process facts. Do not infer a rejection from missing notes or a stale ATS status.

## Workflow
1. Classify the communication: acknowledgement, progress update, delay, preparation, withdrawal confirmation or a rejection already decided by a human. Confirm the triggering event and recipient.
2. State the update plainly, recognise any time already invested and give the next action/date or an honest statement that timing is not yet known. Avoid false promises and invented explanations.
3. Include feedback only when authorised and supported by job-related documented evidence. Do not reveal another applicant's information, private panel discussion or sensitive attributes.
4. Match the HR's tone, preferred length and closing. Respect opt-outs and duplicate-send history; distinguish process notifications from unrelated marketing.
5. Create a send queue. Send only when existing user authorization covers the recipients, content and channel. Recheck current stage before sending; a candidate who has progressed must not receive a stale rejection. Record successful sends, failures and replies separately, and reconcile history before retrying.

## Deliver
`candidate_id | event/source | message type | channel | subject | draft | next action | due date | authorization/status`. Include an overdue-update queue for the recruiter and an exception list for conflicting statuses.

## Check
Names, role and dates are correct. Rejection copy reflects an explicit human decision; feedback is sourced. Each recipient receives only their own information and one appropriate message per event.

Example: "Draft updates for these delayed interviews and approved outcomes in my warm but concise voice, with no invented reasons."

Sources: [CIPD recruitment process](https://www.cipd.org/uk/knowledge/factsheets/recruitment-factsheet/) · [CIPD selection methods](https://www.cipd.org/uk/knowledge/factsheets/selection-factsheet).
