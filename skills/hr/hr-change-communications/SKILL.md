# Plan a people-change rollout

Prepare communications for an approved people change. Inputs: decision record, affected audiences, effective date, owners, consultation requirements supplied by the organisation, and optional `hr-style.md`. Do not infer that a proposal is final.

## Prepare
1. Separate decided facts, proposed changes and unresolved questions. Confirm who may receive each item and when. Do not identify affected employees in general announcements.
2. Map each audience's practical questions: what changes, why, when, required action, support and contact. Use specific dates and time zones. Avoid promises about outcomes or rights not supported by the approved source.
3. Create a brief message sequence for leaders, managers and employees. Keep factual content aligned across channels while adapting detail and tone. Include accessibility/translation needs when known.
4. Draft an FAQ with evidence links. For unknown answers, name the owner and expected update rather than inventing reassurance. Employee concerns become a tracked question log.
5. Produce distribution-ready drafts and a rollout checklist. Send or schedule only when current authorisation covers the audience and final content; record actual delivery separately from preparation.

## Deliver
Message drafts plus `audience | channel | release_time | owner | action | source_version | status`. Include manager talking points, FAQ and a feedback follow-up plan that aggregates concerns without exposing private cases.

**Check:** dates, scope and obligations match the decision record in every version; a manager can answer what happens next. Track delivery evidence and unanswered questions after authorised release.

**Try:** “Turn this approved hybrid-work update into a concise employee note, manager FAQ and rollout checklist in my established style.”
