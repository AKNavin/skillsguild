# Coordinate a cross-border move

Create a coordination plan for a proposed overseas assignment, relocation or cross-border remote arrangement. This is an administrative plan, not immigration, tax or employment-status advice.

## Inputs and work
Use origin/destination jurisdictions, employer entities, proposed dates, physical work locations, assignment terms and authorised HR/legal/payroll contacts. Collect passport or permit documents only through the approved restricted process, not a general AI prompt.

1. Distinguish business travel, remote work, local hire and assignment. Record unresolved classification for the specialist; do not infer permission to work from citizenship, a visa name or past travel.
2. Build parallel workstreams for immigration/right-to-work verification, payroll/tax/social-security review, employment terms, benefits, relocation logistics and data access. Cite current official destination sources where legal details are needed.
3. Back-plan from the proposed start date using verified processing assumptions. Mark estimates and dependencies; an unverified permit or payroll arrangement blocks a claim of readiness.
4. Draft a document request using only the minimum items the authorised specialist requires. Keep document references and review status in the tracker, not identity-document copies.
5. Reconcile the approved arrangement before start, on extension and at return. Record actual approvals, expiry/review dates and repatriation tasks. No application submission or signed commitment is implied by planning.

## Deliver and check
`workstream | jurisdiction | required_check | official_source | owner | dependency | due_date | evidence | status`, plus employee-facing timeline and open decisions. Every readiness statement must trace to owner evidence; unknown processing times remain unknown.

**Try:** “Plan this proposed India-to-UK assignment with dependency owners and questions for our immigration and payroll advisers.”

Source: [GOV.UK new employee coming from abroad (UK example)](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/new-employee-coming-to-work-from-abroad). Checked 2026-10-14; jurisdiction-specific guidance is not a universal legal rule.
