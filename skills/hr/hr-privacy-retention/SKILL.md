# Map HR data and retention

Prepare a practical HR data map and retention review plan. Inputs: systems, record categories, locations/jurisdictions, approved retention schedule, legal-hold process and responsible privacy owner. This skill does not choose a lawful basis or retention period from guesswork.

## Map
1. Inventory applicant, employee, payroll, benefits and case records by purpose, source, system, access roles, transfers and owner. Use category-level examples rather than copying real sensitive records.
2. Record the approved basis/rule and policy version for each purpose. Identify unnecessary fields, duplicate stores and unapproved exports for owner review. A public library or shared prompt is never a storage location for personnel data.
3. Capture the retention trigger (such as case closure or employment end), duration, jurisdiction, authoritative source and exceptions. Do not reuse UK guidance as another country's legal rule; verify the applicable local requirements with the responsible owner.
4. Build a review queue with stable record/group IDs, trigger date, proposed review date and hold status. Unknown triggers, conflicting rules or legal holds block automated disposal. Access restriction and deletion are distinct actions.
5. Draft a least-access matrix and correction/request routing process. Report proposed changes and affected systems; execute only authorised changes with recovery and audit evidence where supported.

## Deliver
`record_category | purpose | system | owner | access | rule_source | trigger | retention | hold_check | next_review`, plus gaps and a minimal action queue. Never publish the completed inventory if it reveals private system or personnel details.

**Check:** each proposed disposition has a dated rule and confirmed hold check. Expired retention alone does not prove destruction occurred; completion needs system evidence.

**Try:** “Map our HR record categories and draft a retention review queue from this approved schedule; flag missing jurisdictions and legal holds.”

Sources: [ICO collecting and keeping employment records (UK)](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/employment/employment-practices-and-data-protection-keeping-employment-records/collecting-and-keeping-employment-records/). Checked 2026-10-14.
