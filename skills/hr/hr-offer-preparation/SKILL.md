# Offer approval pack

## Inputs
Use the recorded human hiring decision, role, employer entity, location, start date, compensation approvals, current template and approval matrix. Optional private `role-brief.md` and `hr-context.md` supply agreed role facts and tone. A missing approval is a blocker to issuance, not permission to assume it.

## Workflow
1. Reconcile role, level, reporting line, working pattern, employment type and start date with the selected candidate record and approved brief. Flag discrepancies with their owner.
2. Separate fixed pay, variable/conditional pay, joining payments, equity and benefits. Preserve currency, pay period, payment conditions and effective dates. Calculate totals only for compatible components; do not describe equity or target bonus as guaranteed cash.
3. Compare the package to approved budget/band and record exceptions for the named approver. Do not infer an acceptable salary from age, gender, family status or previous pay.
4. Populate the current jurisdiction-appropriate template without inventing clauses or legal requirements. Keep conditional checks explicit and supplied legal wording intact; escalate missing or disputed terms to the authorised owner.
5. Prepare candidate-facing copy and a private approval summary. Issuing or modifying an offer requires user authorization covering recipient and final content; never send a draft or mark an unsigned offer accepted.

## Deliver
`term | proposed value | approved source | exception | approver | status`, the offer draft, a total-reward breakdown and a handoff checklist with owners. Separate internal negotiation limits from candidate-facing documents.

## Check
Amounts reconcile; variable pay is labelled; legal entity and dates match. No unresolved placeholder or unapproved exception remains in a version marked ready to issue.

Example: "Prepare an offer approval pack from these approved terms, showing fixed pay separately from target bonus and equity."
