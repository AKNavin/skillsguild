# Job description & advert

## Inputs
Use approved responsibilities, level, terms, application route and employer facts. Read optional private `role-brief.md` and `hr-context.md`; use approved writing examples for tone, not extra requirements or unsupported claims.

## Workflow
1. Start with the role's purpose and expected outcomes, then the actual work and team relationships. Make responsibility and authority consistent with seniority.
2. Separate essential entry capabilities, learnable skills and optional experience. Replace vague "rockstar", "young", "native speaker" or "culture fit" language with relevant tasks or demonstrated proficiency. Do not add age, family, health or other protected-attribute preferences.
3. State confirmed location, working pattern, contract type, reporting line, compensation currency and period, benefits and expected process. Mark missing material facts in the internal review copy; never manufacture them.
4. Produce a fuller internal job description and a concise external advert. Include an accessible application route, deadline with timezone if one exists, and the supplied contact for adjustments.
5. Compare against the approved brief. Highlight material changes for the owner, including requirements introduced by an old template. Follow supplied jurisdiction-specific disclosure rules; where those are missing, identify the item requiring local verification rather than asserting compliance.

## Deliver
An internal job description, publish-ready advert once open facts are resolved, and a short fact-check table: `claim | approved source | unresolved item | owner`. Preserve the HR's tone through sentence length and wording while retaining accurate job standards. Publishing is a separate action.

## Check
A reader can identify the work, must-haves, terms and next step without guessing. No public copy contains internal salary notes, candidate details or unresolved placeholders.

Example: "Write a concise advert and internal job description from this approved operations analyst brief in our straightforward tone."
