# Exit feedback insights

Summarize what leavers reported without treating small samples or personal accounts as proven causes.

## Inputs

Use authorized feedback, cohort/time period, exit counts, survey invitations/responses, approved reporting audience and small-group suppression threshold. Optional private `hr-context.md` supplies tone and decision cadence. If no privacy threshold is supplied, prepare only a restricted analyst draft and ask the data owner before wider reporting.

## Workflow

1. Define voluntary/involuntary, regretted and other exit categories only from supplied definitions. Separate exit interviews from survey responses and administrative reasons; do not merge them into one response denominator.
2. Remove direct identifiers and details that could identify individuals indirectly. Paraphrase uncommon anecdotes; do not publish verbatim quotations or tiny cohort breakdowns without an approved disclosure basis.
3. Code each response into a short, explicit theme set. Preserve contradictory views and distinguish reported experience from corroborated facts. Count respondents mentioning a theme once; show multi-label totals can exceed 100%.
4. Report counts and response coverage alongside percentages. Any turnover rate requires its specified headcount denominator and period; do not compare unlike metrics. Avoid inferring causation, predicting individual resignations or ranking managers from small samples.
5. Propose a few reversible actions with an owner, hypothesis, measure and review date. Compare with existing actions to avoid duplicates; retain evidence references privately. Route specific grievance or safety disclosures through the designated restricted process rather than aggregating them away.

## Deliver

`exit-insights.md`: cohort, response_coverage, theme_counts, suppression_notes, contradictory_evidence, limitations. Add action table: action_id, hypothesis, owner, measure, review_date, status.

## Check

No individual is identifiable in shared output; every claim distinguishes feedback from established fact. Unsupported causal or population-wide conclusions are removed.

## Try it

“Summarize these quarterly exit responses for leadership using our privacy threshold. Show response coverage, recurring themes and three measurable follow-up actions.”

Sources checked 2026-10-14: [CIPD employee turnover and retention](https://prod.cipd.org/en/knowledge/factsheets/turnover-retention-factsheet/). Recheck applicable rules and provider documentation when used.
