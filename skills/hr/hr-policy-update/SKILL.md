# Policy change pack

## Use
Use to revise a workplace policy or prepare its rollout from a defined change request. Produce reviewable policy language and implementation steps, not a blanket legal-compliance claim.

## Inputs
Current version, intended change, jurisdiction, affected worker groups, related contracts and policies, effective-date proposal and policy owner. Use private `hr-context.md` for approved tone and terminology; keep substantive rules grounded in supplied policy or verified local sources.

## Do
1. Compare the request with current clauses. Separate editorial cleanup, process change and changes to rights, pay, working conditions or contractual terms.
2. Draft only necessary revisions. Mark missing business decisions and legal questions explicitly; never silently weaken existing entitlements.
3. Build an impact map across forms, HRIS rules, manager guidance, linked policies and communications. Identify whether consultation, employee representatives or contract review may be needed for the authorized specialist to confirm.
4. Check jurisdiction-specific claims against current official sources when applicable. Record source, date and scope; do not import UK or US rules into another country by analogy.
5. Prepare plain-language “what changes / who it affects / when / what to do” communications and a short FAQ.
6. Create a rollout checklist with approval, version, effective date, training, publication, acknowledgement and review owner. Keep acknowledgement separate from contractual consent. Do not publish, overwrite prior policy or send notices without authorization.

## Deliver
`policy-draft.md`, a clause-by-clause change log and FAQ.
`policy-rollout.csv`: change_id, affected_artifact, required_action, owner, dependency, due_date, approval_status, completion_evidence.

## Check
Every change is requested or clearly proposed, cross-references work, prior versions remain retrievable, and unresolved approval or consultation items are visible.

## Example
“Update this hybrid-work policy for the approved office schedule, showing affected clauses and a rollout checklist for review.”
