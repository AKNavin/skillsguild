# Interview debrief organiser

## Inputs
Use interview notes, stage/criterion mapping, the rubric version and decision owner. Optional private `role-brief.md` defines scope; `hr-context.md` defines meeting style. Work privately and preserve each source note rather than overwriting it.

## Workflow
1. Collect interviewers' independent notes before summarising the discussion. Attribute observations to their source; distinguish quotations, paraphrases, opinions and any already-recorded human ratings.
2. Map evidence to agreed criteria. Keep contradictory observations side by side. Missing or unasked evidence becomes `not observed`, not a negative score.
3. Flag claims grounded in rapport, accent, appearance, prestige, guessed personality or protected characteristics as outside the job rubric. Do not infer sensitive traits or convert them into a hiring signal.
4. Identify the smallest clarification that would resolve a material evidence gap. Recommend process actions, such as checking a note or using an equivalent follow-up question, rather than candidate outcomes.
5. Prepare an agenda covering essential evidence gaps first, then disagreements and owner decisions. Record only decisions explicitly made by authorised human reviewers; do not fabricate consensus, average uncalibrated ratings or generate a recommendation to hire/reject.

## Deliver
A packet with `criterion | attributed evidence | source | contradiction/gap | clarification owner`, a debrief agenda and a blank decision log: `human decision | decision maker | stated rationale | follow-up | date`. Preserve different viewpoints even if the group's final decision differs.

## Check
Every candidate-related statement traces to a note. New criteria have not appeared after the interview. Presentation may match the HR's style; evidence standards and decision authority remain fixed.

Example: "Turn these anonymised interview notes into a debrief packet, keeping disagreements visible and leaving the hiring decision to the panel."

Sources: [OPM structured interviews](https://www.opm.gov/policy-data-oversight/assessment-and-selection/structured-interviews/) · [CIPD selection methods](https://www.cipd.org/uk/knowledge/factsheets/selection-factsheet).
