# Policy answer with evidence

Give a useful policy answer that the employee and policy owner can verify.

## Inputs

Use the exact question, relevant location/employment type, event date and approved policy corpus with owner, version, effective date and scope. Optional private `hr-context.md` supplies the HR team's language and formatting. Ask for only facts that change the applicable rule; never request an entire personnel file.

## Workflow

1. Determine which policy covers the event date, jurisdiction and worker category. Check amendments and precedence between corporate, local and contract-specific rules; do not apply the newest document automatically to an earlier event.
2. Extract the controlling sections with page/section references. Distinguish an explicit rule, an example, discretionary language and silence. When documents conflict, present the conflict to the named owner instead of manufacturing a blended rule.
3. Answer in plain language: what the policy states, what the employee needs to do, where to submit it and what remains uncertain. Quote only the smallest necessary passage and link the source.
4. Separate policy explanation from eligibility, entitlement, legal or benefit approval. Missing facts or exceptions become a focused question and owner referral; don't convert an example into a guaranteed result.
5. Draft in the approved HR voice while preserving material conditions. Date the answer and list the sources actually consulted. Prepare it for the chosen private channel; do not send automatically or add personal case details to a shared knowledge base.

## Deliver

A short answer plus `evidence`: policy_title, version, section, effective_from, jurisdiction, source_link. End with next_action, owner and unresolved_question when needed.

## Check

Every substantive rule is traceable to an applicable source. An absent rule is labelled “not specified,” not invented.

## Try it

“Answer whether this employee can carry leave into next year using the attached India policy and amendment. Cite the applicable sections and flag any approval needed.”

Sources checked 2026-10-14: [ICO employment records guidance (UK)](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/employment/employment-practices-and-data-protection-keeping-employment-records/). Recheck applicable rules and provider documentation when used.
