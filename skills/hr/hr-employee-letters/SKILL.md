# Employee letter drafts

Prepare a factual employee letter that matches the HR team's style and approved wording.

## Inputs

Use the requested purpose, recipient, authorized disclosure scope, approved template, verified dates/job title, relevant employment entity, jurisdiction and signatory. Optional private `hr-context.md` supplies style, salutation and formatting; `role-brief.md` can clarify official role wording. Do not include salary, address or other personal information unless required and authorized.

## Workflow

1. Choose the correct letter type: employment confirmation, experience/service letter, routine acknowledgment or a factual reference. Contract variations, termination and disputed matters require the organization's approved specialist wording; do not improvise legal clauses.
2. Map every variable to a verified source and effective date. Resolve contradictions between request, HRIS and documents. Use clearly marked placeholders for missing facts in drafts; never fill them with guesses.
3. Preserve approved substantive clauses exactly; adapt only tone and formatting within the allowed template. Keep factual employment confirmation distinct from performance endorsement or future guarantees.
4. Check recipient-specific disclosure, correct entity/name, chronology, current title and signatory authority. For references, distinguish a fact from an evidenced opinion; exclude irrelevant sensitive personal information. Acas guidance cited here applies to the UK, not every jurisdiction.
5. Produce a clean draft and a short verification list. Stamp document version, generated date and source references in the internal cover note. Do not invent a signature, issue the letter or send it automatically.

## Deliver

`letter-draft.md` plus cover note: purpose, recipient, template_version, jurisdiction, field_sources, missing_fields, authorized_signatory, review_status. Keep internal evidence out of the recipient-facing letter.

## Check

No unresolved placeholder remains in an “issue-ready” draft. Every assertion is within the requested purpose and verified disclosure scope.

## Try it

“Draft an employment confirmation using our approved template and this verified record. Keep our concise HR tone and list anything the signatory must confirm.”

Sources checked 2026-10-14: [Acas factual employment references (UK)](https://www.acas.org.uk/providing-a-job-reference/what-employers-can-say-in-a-reference). Recheck applicable rules and provider documentation when used.
