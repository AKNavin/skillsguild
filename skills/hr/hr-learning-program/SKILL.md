# Learning program builder

## Use
Use to design or refresh a learning program after the need is clear. Produce material a facilitator and learner can use immediately.

## Inputs
Target work tasks, audience and starting level, baseline examples, duration, delivery channel, accessibility needs and budget. Read private `hr-context.md` and relevant `role-brief.md` if provided. Do not claim a generic course solves an unvalidated business problem.

## Do
1. Translate each outcome into an observable demonstration. Specify the work sample, criteria and acceptable evidence before writing lessons.
2. Build a short sequence of explanation, worked example, learner practice and feedback. Reuse authorized internal examples with personal or customer details removed.
3. Allocate minutes, facilitator preparation, materials and practice support. Offer an accessible alternative where the requested format creates a barrier.
4. Create a baseline activity and a comparable end activity. Keep the rubric focused on the work; do not label a learner incompetent from attendance or quiz scores.
5. Add an on-the-job assignment and manager follow-up date. Capture transfer evidence separately from attendance, satisfaction and immediate assessment.
6. Prepare the invitation and feedback form as drafts unless sending is already authorized. Flag resource costs and capacity constraints.

## Deliver
`program.md`: agenda, facilitator notes, learner exercises and answer guidance.
`evaluation.csv`: outcome_id, baseline_task, final_task, criterion, evidence, workplace_follow_up, owner, date.
Add materials and an implementation checklist.

## Check
Timing adds up, each outcome is practiced and assessed, and the follow-up can show application without claiming causal business impact from a simple before/after comparison.

## Example
“Build a 90-minute interview-training workshop with role play, an observation rubric and a two-week workplace follow-up.”
