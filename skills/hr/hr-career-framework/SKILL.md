# Career level framework

## Use
Use to create or simplify a role-family framework for development and career conversations. Describe levels of work; do not assign people to levels automatically.

## Inputs
Role family, current responsibilities, approved level names, example work and existing frameworks. Optional private `hr-context.md` supplies terminology; `role-brief.md` supplies role outcomes and boundaries. Separate proposed additions from approved expectations.

## Do
1. Identify a small set of dimensions that genuinely change with role scope: problem complexity, autonomy, impact, collaboration and relevant craft skills.
2. For each dimension, describe observable work at each level. Replace vague labels such as “executive presence” with examples tied to job requirements.
3. Distinguish specialist and management pathways when both exist. Avoid requiring people management for progression in a specialist track.
4. Include example artifacts that could evidence each expectation and clarify whether an example is illustrative rather than mandatory.
5. Check adjacent levels for meaningful differences and missing steps. Avoid arbitrary tenure, age, hours worked or personality proxies.
6. Produce a draft mapping of roles to scope for job owners to validate. Promotion, pay and individual leveling remain separate human decisions.

## Deliver
`career-framework.csv`: role_family, level, dimension, observable_expectation, example_evidence, boundary_from_previous_level, draft_status.
Add `framework-guide.md` with intended use, specialist/manager paths, gaps and validation owners.

## Check
A reviewer can explain how neighboring levels differ using work examples. The framework permits different ways to demonstrate a capability and contains no inferred employee ratings.

## Example
“Create a four-level customer-success framework from these responsibilities, with separate specialist and manager paths and evidence examples.”

Sources: [CIPD competence and competency frameworks](https://www.cipd.org/en/knowledge/factsheets/competency-factsheet). Checked 2026-10-14.
