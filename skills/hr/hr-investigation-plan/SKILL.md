# Investigation planning pack

## Use
Use after an authorized person decides an investigation is needed. Prepare the plan for a qualified, impartial investigator; do not make findings or disciplinary recommendations.

## Inputs
Case ID, allegations, scope, jurisdiction, applicable procedure, investigator, known evidence and permitted access. Store the pack in the restricted case workspace. Confirm who receives the report and whether the investigator may make recommendations.

## Do
1. Draft terms of reference: questions to establish, exclusions, reporting line, available authority, timetable and conflicts check. Escalate investigator conflicts to the appointing owner.
2. Break allegations into factual questions. List potentially supporting and contradicting evidence with custodians and preservation deadlines; include evidence that could disconfirm an allegation.
3. Plan neutral interviews, using open questions followed by specific clarifications. Separate firsthand observation from hearsay. Include an opportunity for the subject to respond to the allegations through the applicable process.
4. Define evidence handling: original location, version, collection authority, access log and retention owner. Do not access personal accounts, record interviews or contact witnesses without authorization.
5. Draft notices and interview records with confidentiality limits and the appropriate support or representation arrangements from verified policy. Do not invent jurisdiction-wide rights or deadlines.
6. Track outstanding evidence and revised dates. Keep facts, disputed statements and unresolved gaps separate; send legal or complex procedural questions to the designated specialist.

## Deliver
`investigation-plan.md`: terms, interviews, sequence, communications and review points.
Restricted `evidence-matrix.csv`: allegation_id, factual_question, evidence_needed, supporting_or_contradicting, custodian, collection_authority, due_date, status, limitation.

## Check
Scope is authorized, each allegation has a fair evidence plan, conflicts are handled, and no conclusion is prewritten.

## Example
“Prepare a neutral investigation plan for this approved grievance scope, including evidence preservation and questions for each interview.”
