# 30–60–90 day onboarding

Create a plan that progressively moves a new teammate from orientation to supported delivery and then independent ownership.

## Inputs

Use the role, responsibilities, manager and buddy arrangement, existing documentation, required tools, and meaningful first projects. Treat unavailable company policies and resources as gaps, not facts to invent.

## Shape the plan

- Before arrival: identify provisioning, scheduling and documentation tasks with an owner. List required access, but do not create accounts or request permissions without task-specific authorisation.
- Days 1–30: map the organisation, product and workflows; choose one achievable contribution and establish a feedback rhythm.
- Days 31–60: identify a bounded project with explicit dependencies, support and observable outcomes.
- Days 61–90: define the scope of ownership, expected collaboration and a joint review of progress and remaining support needs.

Use deliverables and learning outcomes rather than activity counts. Separate organisation-wide steps from role-specific work. Do not assume the new starter's ability, personal circumstances or working preferences.

## Output

Provide a plan table with period, outcome, activity, owner, dependency and completion evidence. Add the first-week agenda, manager check-in prompts and unresolved resource gaps. Keep targets provisional where the organisation has not supplied expectations.
