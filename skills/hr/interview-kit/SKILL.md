# Structured interview kit

## Inputs
Use responsibilities, level, interview duration and required capabilities. Read optional private `role-brief.md`, `hr-context.md` and an existing rubric. Preserve approved standards; resolve disagreements between the brief and rubric before evaluating people.

## Workflow
1. Map essential capabilities to the work they enable. Assign this interview a limited purpose so it does not repeat evidence collected elsewhere.
2. Write a behavioural question or realistic scenario for each capability, with permitted neutral probes. Keep core questions, order and assessment standards consistent across candidates; allow clarifying follow-ups and agreed access adjustments.
3. Define evidence to record and behavioural anchors. Separate insufficient evidence from `not observed`; avoid impressions such as confidence, charm or "culture fit" unless converted into an established job task.
4. Allocate time for introduction, questions, candidate questions and close. Provide the interviewer with an opening explaining format, note-taking and the supplied adjustment route.
5. Add one synthetic calibration response to demonstrate an observation versus an inference. Give interviewers a private note template for independent evidence recording before discussion. Do not infer sensitive characteristics from names, voice, accent, appearance or personal circumstances.

## Deliver
`interview stage | competency | role responsibility | question | permitted probes | evidence to record | anchor descriptions | minutes`; a timed agenda; interviewer notes template; and candidate preparation text. Adapt the HR's tone in the introduction and closing, never the required standard. This kit supports human assessment and does not decide hiring outcomes.

## Check
The agenda fits the time limit. Every question has a job-related purpose and usable anchors; no critical competency is assessed only through unstructured conversation. The candidate's questions have protected time.

Example: "Build a 45-minute structured interview for this payroll role, with clear probes and an evidence-note template."
