# Personalised candidate outreach

## Inputs
Use approved role facts, a relevant professional profile or work sample, channel limits and contact preferences. Optional private `hr-context.md` supplies approved tone; `role-brief.md` supplies role scope and terms. Keep private profile data out of public skill files.

## Workflow
1. Check the recipient and role, duplicates, previous contact and any opt-out. Use only professional evidence available through authorised access. Do not infer identity traits, interests or personal circumstances from names, photos or background.
2. Connect one concrete piece of work evidence to one responsibility. If there is no defensible connection, write straightforward role-based outreach without invented praise.
3. State why the role could be relevant, confirmed location/working pattern and compensation if approved. End with one low-friction next step. Do not imply that the person has been selected or guarantee an outcome.
4. Match the HR's approved greeting, length, formality and closing. Do not copy private facts from style examples or personalise selection standards.
5. Draft a bounded follow-up sequence with timing, a new useful detail and a stop condition for replies, opt-outs or the final attempt. Sending requires existing user authorization covering the recipients, content and channel; when absent, deliver drafts.

## Deliver
`recipient_id | evidence source | role connection | channel | subject | message | follow-up date | stop condition | status`. Add unresolved facts and suggested edits to the tone profile only when supported by explicit feedback; do not silently store candidate details there.

## Check
Every personalised claim has a source. Links and dates resolve to the right vacancy; there is one clear call to action and no contact for suppressed recipients.

Example: "Draft an initial message and one follow-up for these approved profiles using my concise, friendly style."

Sources: [CIPD recruitment process](https://www.cipd.org/uk/knowledge/factsheets/recruitment-factsheet/) · [CIPD selection methods](https://www.cipd.org/uk/knowledge/factsheets/selection-factsheet).
